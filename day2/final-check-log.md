- **2000 corners won 37 of 50 head-to-head runs (74%).** The chance of winning 37 or more of 50 by pure luck (coin-flip odds) is 0.00047, about 1 in 2,100, so the improvement is real. Wins are counted on the full-precision scores; one MH05 pair tied at 5.68 cm to two decimals.
- **Better on all 5 flights, about 10% on average** (9, 17, 16, 2, 4%).
- **With the change, ORB-SLAM3 on Benny's PC beats the paper's published number on all 5 flights.** Normal ORB-SLAM3 on his PC is slightly behind the paper on MH01/MH02 (+0.2/+0.3 cm) and ahead on MH03-05.
- **The surprise:** the biggest gains are on the easy and medium flights (MH02 -17%, MH03 -16%), which were *not* used for tuning. On the hard, dark flights it was tuned for, the gain shrank from the tuning-phase -0.18 / -0.37 cm to -0.08 / -0.22 cm. That's a classic sign that part of a tuning-phase gain was luck, and exactly why a fresh final check matters. Likely reason: on easy flights the extra corners are good, well-lit corners that tighten every position estimate; on the dark flights the extra corners are weaker, so they help less.
- **Session-to-session caveat:** normal's MH02 middle was 2.28 cm in the earlier session but 3.59 cm in the final check, with the same program and the same run speed. Something about a session (PC state, timing) shifts results. That's why the final comparison only uses the two versions run side by side, alternating, in the same session.
- Run speed: the same for both versions (e.g. MH04 ~110 s), so the improvement isn't from extra thinking time.
